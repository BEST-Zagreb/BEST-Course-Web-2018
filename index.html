LBG Zagreb's Spring Course 2018
News about the course

Welcome to the official website of LBG Zagreb's, yet-to-be epic Spring Course: GAS what's cooking!

Have you ever been amazed by the way people are using oil, gas, sun or wind to get energy? Or by the way they are using that exact energy for things from boiling your water for coffe to making massive industrial machines work? Have you ever thought "Huh, it would be really cool to know how my car is able to run on a tiny dead bacteria"? Have you wondered what is the magic behind mesmerizing solar panels and windmills? Ever wondered what is the meaning of life? If the answer to those questions is yes, then let me hear a loud "HELLOOOOOOOOO ZAGREB!!", because it might just be you who gets the chance to spend 10 unforgettable days in the beautiful capital of Croatia! Not only you will find out what the real BEST spirit is, you will also be able to taste some of the best Croatian ones :D You can have the time of your life experiencing rich Croatian culture and meeting some of the friendliest people and at the same time learning something amazing. If you are interested in all mentioned above and ready to sacrifice few hours of sleep for the BEST time of your life, don't be shy, just apply! LBG Zagreb is waiting for you! :) Our course partner INA, d.d. is a medium-sized European oil company with a leading role in Croatian oil business and a strong position in the region in the oil and gas exploration and production, oil processing, and oil and oil products distribution activities.
